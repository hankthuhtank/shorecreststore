From shore
to crest.

The shore is the lowest ground there is. The crest is the top of the ridge. Shorecrest lives in between: for anyone tired of the same routine, and for anyone who hit bottom and found their way back up, one view at a time.

It began with 45 days on the road. Mountains, ocean, long drives, and quiet places that made the rest of life feel a little smaller. Every piece here starts with a photograph from that trip.
